Our Journey — Born out of Passion

We have been developing the software since 2024 and never stopped a single day on improving it. Back then we were using Hammer, which was pretty great at the start, and they dumped their customer because of greed. They released another version using the excuse of a Steam update to x64 bits. Nevertheless, we are thankful because of their pioneering of this method of pirating.

That's when we said: it's enough, it's time to make our own tool. And thus we started developing since early 2024, starting with 2,000 games. We adapted, looked at other tools, and thought we could parody other tools — hence the name Wannabe spelt ONENNABE. And now here we are.

To the OG testers out there: thank you.
