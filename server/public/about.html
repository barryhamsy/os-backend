Our Journey & Story

Continuous evolution, driven by passion and dedication.

We have been developing the software since 2024 and have never stopped a single day on improving it. Back then, we were using Hammer, which was pretty great at the start, but they eventually dumped their customer base due to greed. They released another version under the excuse of a Steam update to x64 bits. Nevertheless, we remain thankful for their pioneering work in this method.

"That's when we said: it's enough, it's time to make our own tool."

And thus, we started developing in early 2024, starting with a library of only 2,000 games. We adapted, observed other tools, and thought we could parody them—hence the name Wannabe, stylized as Onennabe.

Now here we are today. To all the OG testers out there who stuck with us from day one: Thank you.

Keep sailing... pirates! 🏴‍☠️
