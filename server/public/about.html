🏴‍☠️ The Story of ONENNABE

We have been actively developing this software since early 2024 and have never stopped a single day on improving and expanding it for our community.

Back then, we were using Hammer, which was pretty great at the start. However, they eventually dumped their customer base due to greed, releasing another paid version under the excuse of a Steam update to x64 bits. Nevertheless, we remain thankful for their early pioneering in this method of game preservation.

That's when we said: "It's enough. It's time to make our own tool."

And thus, we started developing from scratch in early 2024, starting with a library of only 2,000 games. We adapted, analyzed other tools, and thought: "We can parody existing software, hence the name Wannabe — spelt ONENNABE."

And now, here we are! To all the OG testers out there who stuck with us from day one — thank you!

Keep sailing... pirates. ⚓🏴‍☠️
