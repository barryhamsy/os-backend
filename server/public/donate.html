Support & Donations

Help us keep the servers running and project alive.

You are not obligated to donate or pay extra. However, if you feel like supporting our project and appreciate all the hard work that goes into this app, any contribution is deeply meaningful to us.

We know you paid a substantial amount for a one-time lifetime license, but all of that revenue goes directly into game sources, active development, and high server costs. On rainy days when sales are slow and server bills arrive, I often have to fund costs out of my own personal salary to keep the services online.

Thank you so much to anyone who chooses to support us! ❤️
